Mini Hold'em Solver

Each cell holds the two-card combos of one hand: suited above the diagonal, offsuit below, pairs on it. In Mini every cell is a single combo. Colours show how often the solver takes each action, averaged over the cell's combos that are still in range. With "bar height shows range" on, the filled height is the share of the cell's combos the player still holds at this point. Hatched cells can't be dealt on this board.
