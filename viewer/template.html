river-delta

Each cell shows the EV of its combos at this decision, in chips: what they win from the pot by the end of the hand, minus the chips put in after the blinds, against the opponent's range here with both players following the solve's strategy. The blinds count as dead money, so folding without having bet is worth 0. A cell averages its combos by how often each one is here, card removal included. Stronger violet means higher EV; the scale runs from the lowest EV (or 0) to the highest. Hatched cells can't be dealt on this board.

Each cell holds the two-card combos of one hand: suited above the diagonal, offsuit below, pairs on it. Colours show how often each action is taken, averaged over the cell's combos that are still in range; bigger bets are darker. With "bar height shows range" on, the filled height is the share of the cell's combos the player still holds at this point. Hatched cells can't be dealt on this board.
